❓ CLEP® Calculus Simulator — Help & Guide

📘 About the Application

The CLEP® Calculus Practice Exam Simulator is an interactive web application designed to help students master single-variable calculus and prepare for the official College Board CLEP® Calculus exam. Features include infinite procedurally generated practice problems, step-by-step solutions formatted in KaTeX LaTeX, section timing controls, and comprehensive score history tracking.

🏛️ About the Official CLEP® Calculus Exam

The College Board CLEP® Calculus exam tests single-variable calculus concepts typically covered in a first-semester college calculus course.

  • Total Exam Duration: 90 Minutes (44 Multiple-Choice Questions).
  • Section 1 (Non-Calculator): 27 Questions | 50 Minutes — Calculator Prohibited.
  • Section 2 (Calculator Permitted): 17 Questions | 40 Minutes — Physical CLEP®-approved graphing calculator (e.g., TI-84 Plus CE) permitted.
  • Topic Weighting: Limits & Continuity (~10%), Differential Calculus (~50%), Integral Calculus (~40%).

📊 Exam Scoring & Scaled Score (20–80)

Official CLEP® exams do not report scores as simple percentages. Instead, raw scores (number of correct answers) are converted into a scaled score ranging from 20 to 80 based on College Board difficulty equivalence curves.

  • Passing Score: A scaled score of 50 (typically around 50–55% raw correct) is recommended by ACE for earning 3 to 4 college credits.
  • No Guessing Penalty: Points are awarded for correct answers only. Unanswered questions receive 0 points, so you should answer every question.

⚙️ Difficulty Levels Explained

🟢 Easy
Focuses on core fundamental calculus operations, smaller integer constants, and straightforward 1-step calculus rules. Ideal for initial practice and topic warm-ups.
🟡 Medium (Default)
Directly mirrors the difficulty, coefficient distributions, and multi-step reasoning of the official College Board CLEP® Calculus exam.
🔴 Hard
Challenge mode featuring complex composite functions, broader parameter ranges, trickier algebraic distractors, and higher-order derivative/integral rules.
🔥 Extreme
Mastery challenge mode featuring multi-layered chain rules, intricate implicit curves, advanced integration by parts, washer volumes, non-elementary numerical integrals, and rigorous analytical calculus problems.

🧪 Test Modes Explained

⏱️ Exam Mode
Simulates official exam conditions. Features a strict 90-minute timed limit across 2 sections, locked answer choices, disabled instant hints/solutions, and detailed final diagnostic results with scaled 20–80 scores.
💡 Practice Mode
Interactive learning environment featuring a count-up timer starting at 00:00 (timer turns red in overtime past the section limit without auto-submitting). Features instant answer verification (✓ Check Answer), step-by-step KaTeX LaTeX solution explanations, contextual hints (💡 Hint), and a pause session timer (⏸ Pause).
